House of Hollow

House of Hollow is a gothic horror novel by Krystal Sutherland. It follows the story of three sisters who were born with hollow eyes and the mystery surrounding their past and their family.

The Only One Left

The Only One Left by Riley Sager is a gothic suspense novel set between a decaying cliffside mansion in 1983 Maine and a buried family tragedy from 1929. Sager builds the story around rumor, class tension, old money, and the fear of living beside someone the world has already judged a killer.

Atomic Habits

Atomic Habits is a nonfiction book written by James Clear. Released in 2018, it tells the reader how to improve their life by breaking bad habits

Gangsta Granny

Gangsta Granny is a children’s book written by David Walliams, first published by Harper Collins in 2011. It is illustrated by Tony Ross and tells the story of a boy and his granny on a daring and hilarious mission to steal the Crown Jewels
